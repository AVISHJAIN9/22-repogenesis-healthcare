# Healthcare & Technical Glossary

- **EMR**: Electronic Medical Record, the digital equivalent of a patient's paper chart.
- **CDSS**: Clinical Decision Support System, algorithmic software that assists clinicians with diagnostic and therapeutic decisions.
- **DICOM**: Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine, the international standard for medical images.
- **RBAC**: Role-Based Access Control, restricting system access based on clinical roles (Doctor, Nurse, Admin).
- **TPA**: Third-Party Administrator, organization processing health insurance claims.
- **HIPAA**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, setting data privacy regulations for medical records.
